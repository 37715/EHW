Fencing

Garden fencing, built to stay up.

What we put up

A boundary that does the job.

New garden fences

A new run where there wasn’t one, or where the old one has finally given up. Posts set properly, panels or boards fixed square, the line kept straight.

Panels & posts

Blown panels and rotten posts replaced without rebuilding a fence that still has life in it. We replace what’s failed and leave the sound parts alone.

Feather-edge & closeboard

Feather-edge and closeboard where you want a solid boundary rather than a panel that flaps. The boards overlap, the posts are concreted, and the top line stays level.

Storm damage

Fences that went over in a Hampshire gale. We clear the wreckage, set new posts where the old ones have snapped or rotted, and put the boundary back.

Gates

Garden gates hung on the new or existing run, so the fence has a way through that actually closes. Posts either side set to take the weight.

The price

We quote the fence, not an hourly rate.

We come and look at the run: length, height, what’s still sound, access, and whether the old timber has to come away. Then you get a fixed price for the whole job. That’s the figure you pay.

Quotes are free, and we usually get out to look within a few days. If a fence has gone into something we shouldn’t be cutting or digging near, we’ll say so rather than guess.

Where we work

Hayling Island, Havant, Emsworth, Waterlooville.

Home is Hayling Island. Havant and Emsworth are over the bridge. Waterlooville is a regular run for us. If you’re just outside that — Cosham, Bedhampton, Purbrook — ring and we’ll tell you whether we can get there.
